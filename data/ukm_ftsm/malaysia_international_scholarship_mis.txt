Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) - Guide for Prospective UKM Students

Source: Ministry of Higher Education Malaysia (MOHE) MIS 2026 official guidelines and FAQ, retrieved 2026-10-11. UKM is one of the participating public universities.
Official application portal: https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER/index.php

1. What MIS is

MIS is a Malaysian Government (MOHE) initiative to attract the best international talents to pursue full-time postgraduate studies (Master's and PhD) at leading Malaysian universities, including UKM. It is postgraduate only - it does not cover bachelor's study.

2. What MIS covers

- Full tuition coverage at participating universities.
- Monthly living allowance (RM1,500 per month in recent cycles, paid against progress reports).
- Health insurance.
- Return airfare (per cycle terms).
- Note: exact benefit items are confirmed in each year's official guidelines.

3. Who can apply

- Citizens of MIS recipient countries (including China; ASEAN and Commonwealth countries are included - China applicants are eligible).
- Applicants intending to pursue full-time Master's or PhD studies in Malaysia; or students already pursuing full-time postgraduate studies at Malaysian public universities with at least two (2) semesters remaining before the end of study.
- Age limit: not more than 40 years old at application for Master's; not more than 45 for PhD.
- English proficiency: IELTS band 6.0 or higher, or TOEFL 550 (paper-based) or higher, or previous degree(s) taught in English.
- No application fee.

4. Academic requirements

- Master's applicants: CGPA 3.00 or 2nd Class Upper (Honours) or higher at bachelor's level.
- PhD applicants: CGPA 3.00 or excellent results at Master's level in courses related to the intended PhD study.
- Research proposal (minimum 1,000 words) required for Masters by Research and PhD applicants.

5. Priority areas of study

Priority is given to (ICT and Computer Science are included, so FTSM programmes qualify):
- Information and Communication Technologies
- Engineering, Manufacturing and Construction
- Natural Sciences, Mathematics and Statistics
- Education; Arts and Humanities; Social Sciences, Journalism and Information
- Business, Administration and Law
- Agriculture, Forestry, Fisheries and Veterinary
- Health and Welfare (not including medicine, nursing and pharmacy)

6. Required documents (uploaded as PDF, in English)

- Certified copy of passport (minimum 6 months validity).
- Certified academic transcripts (undergraduate; plus Master's transcript for PhD applicants).
- Grade-system explanation letter from alma mater if the transcript lacks CGPA/marks.
- English proficiency test result (IELTS/TOEFL) or official letter confirming English as medium of instruction.
- Two recommendation letters (lecturers/supervisors/superiors; not family or close friends).
- Admission letter to a Malaysian university (if already admitted or currently studying).
- Curriculum Vitae.
- Research proposal (Masters by Research and PhD only).

7. How and when to apply

- Apply online only at https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER/index.php.
- The application window is announced per cycle in the MIS guidelines (recent cycles opened around May and closed around June/July; MIS 2026 applications closed 3 April 2026).
- Selection: document screening, then shortlisted applicants are invited for an online interview.
- Re-applying in a new cycle counts as a fresh application.

8. MTCP Scholarship (related government scheme)

The Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme (MTCP) Scholarship is a similar government scholarship for citizens of MTCP recipient countries, covering tuition and allowances for postgraduate study; application is at https://mtcp.kln.gov.my. UKM is a participating university.

9. Contact for questions

- MIS: through the MOHE online application system.
- UKM Graduate Centre can advise on admission steps after scholarship nomination: graduate@ukm.edu.my.
