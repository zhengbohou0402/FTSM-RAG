UKM Scholarships and Financial Aid (Official Information)

Source: UKM Graduate Centre (Pusat Siswazah) and UKM official pages, retrieved 2026-10-11.
Contact: UKM Graduate Centre, email awardsandscholarship@ukm.edu.my / graduate@ukm.edu.my.

1. UKM Vice-Chancellor's Scholarship (Gold and Silver)

The Vice-Chancellor's Scholarship is offered to outstanding local and international students pursuing Masters and Doctoral degrees at UKM. It is open to new prospective postgraduate students who have registered online, for full-time research or mixed mode (minimum research:coursework ratio 70:30). Applicants must not be receiving any other scholarship, sponsorship, salary or allowance.

1.1 Gold Award benefits
- Tuition fees waived every semester; registration, service and activities fees (including thesis and convocation) waived.
- Living allowance: RM3,200 per month for PhD, RM2,200 per month for Masters.
- Accommodation allowance: RM300 per month.
- One round-trip economy flight ticket to/from KLIA for international, Sabahan and Sarawakian candidates.
- Visa costs covered for international students, throughout postgraduate studies.

1.2 Gold Award eligibility
- Masters applicants: Bachelor's degree with Distinction, CGPA at least 3.67 or First Class Honours or equivalent from a reputable university.
- PhD applicants: Master's degree or equivalent, and at least TWO published articles in Q2 journals or above as first student author.
- Fast-track PhD: criteria as per the Masters entry requirement above.

1.3 Silver Award benefits
- Registration and tuition fees waived.
- Living allowance: RM2,000 per month for PhD, RM1,500 per month for Masters.
- Accommodation allowance: RM300 per month.

1.4 Silver Award eligibility
- Masters applicants: same academic requirement as Gold (CGPA 3.67 / First Class Honours).
- PhD applicants: Master's degree, with at least TWO published articles in Q3 journals or above as first student author.

1.5 Application procedure
- Download the application form from the UKM Graduate Centre (Pusat Siswazah) website, complete it and email it with supporting documents (passport copy, photo, transcripts, publication front pages, research proposal, CV) to graduate@ukm.edu.my / awardsandscholarship@ukm.edu.my.
- Applications are evaluated by the University Selection Committee; recipients must maintain performance against a Key Performance Index (KPI) or the scholarship may be terminated.

2. Postgraduate scholarship and award calendar at UKM

According to the UKM financial aid page (ivi.ukm.my), the recurring opportunities are:
- Gold Award Nomination, UKM Graduate Centre: opens May - August every year.
- MTDC Innovation Book Award, UKM Graduate Centre: opens June - August every year.
- UKM Vice-Chancellor Scholarship and UKM Zakat Scholarship, UKM Graduate Centre: opens July every year.
- Skim Zamalah Penyelidikan (Research Assistantship Scheme), UKM Graduate Centre: opens August every year.
- Zakat Universiti: open all year round (https://www.ukm.my/zakat/).

3. Other scholarships and sponsorships advertised via UKM Graduate Centre

The UKM Graduate Centre "Other Scholarships" page lists, among others:

- MyBrainSC (KPT) - Malaysian Ministry of Education scholarship for local students.
- Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme (MTCP) Scholarship - for citizens of MTCP recipient countries.
- OSRAM Sponsorship Program for Master's and PhD at UKM.
- Graduate Excellence Programme (GREP) Scholarship.
- HLAF Science and Technology Scholarship and Grant.
- Bank Negara Malaysia Scholarship Award (Master and PhD).
- Khazanah Watan Scholarship Programme.
- Biasiswa Sukan Persekutuan (Federal Sports Scholarship).

Note: availability and windows for external scholarships change each year; always check the UKM Graduate Centre pages (https://www.ukm.my/pusatsiswazah/) for the current cycle.

4. Practical notes for international FTSM students

- University-level scholarships (Vice-Chancellor, MIS, MTCP) are administered centrally, not by FTSM; the faculty itself mainly offers Graduate Research Assistant (GRA) positions tied to lecturers' research grants.
- Research-mode postgraduate students are usually nominated through their supervisor/faculty for Vice-Chancellor and Zamalah schemes.
- For the latest deadline of each scheme, consult the UKM Graduate Centre website or email awardsandscholarship@ukm.edu.my.
