    How the assistant answers

    Every reply goes through the same retrieval pipeline — and questions the knowledge base cannot support are rejected instead of guessed.

    01

    Ingest

    Official FTSM pages, timetables and curated guides are crawled, split into chunks and embedded into the vector index.

    02

    Hybrid retrieve

    Dense vector search and BM25 keyword search run in parallel, then fuse through Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

    03

    Rerank

    A cross-encoder reranker promotes the most relevant chunks before anything reaches the model.

    04

    Ground & answer

    The model answers only from retrieved context. Weak evidence fails a dual-threshold gate — no hallucinated answers.
